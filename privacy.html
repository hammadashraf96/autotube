AutoTube privacy policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Who uses AutoTube

AutoTube is used only by its owner, for the owner's own YouTube channels. It is not offered to other people.

What data it accesses

When the owner signs in with Google, AutoTube receives permission to manage the owner's YouTube channels (upload and update videos, captions and thumbnails) and to read their YouTube statistics and analytics.

How the data is used and stored

  • The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's own computer and is never shared.
  • Video statistics are used only to show the owner how their videos perform.
  • AutoTube does not collect, sell or share data about any other person, and it does not use advertising or tracking.

AutoTube's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. AutoTube uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
